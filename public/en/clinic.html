The clinic where treatment takes place

Treatment is provided at Özel DentTEK Ağız ve Diş Sağlığı Polikliniği (Polyclinic for Oral and Dental Health). The polyclinic is in the Cumhuriyet neighbourhood of Esenyurt, at Altıntaş Okutan Park on Nazım Hikmet Boulevard.

Hygiene and sterilisation

  1. 1
    A separate sterile set for every patient

    Hand instruments that enter the mouth are packaged for each patient; package integrity and indicator colour are checked before use.

  2. 2
    Recorded autoclave cycles

    Sterilisation is carried out in a steam autoclave; cycles are monitored with chemical indicators and regular biological testing, and are recorded.

  3. 3
    Single-use materials

    Needle tips, gloves, masks, gowns, suction tips, cups and similar items are single-use.

  4. 4
    Surface and unit disinfection

    After every patient the dental unit, work surfaces and handles are disinfected; water lines are cleaned regularly.

  5. 5
    Medical waste management

    Sharps and medical waste are collected separately and handed over for disposal in accordance with the regulations.

Main equipment available at the clinic

  • Digital panoramic X-ray — planning and follow-up images are taken at the clinic
  • Three-dimensional imaging (CBCT tomography) by referral in cases where it is needed
  • Digital impressions and collaboration with the dental laboratory (zirconia, veneers, implant-supported prostheses)
  • Surgical and implant motor; separate preparation and sterilisation area
  • Accessibility: ground-floor entrance, no steps, accessible toilet

When you come for an examination

  • Bring your most recent X-rays and any documents from previous treatment, if you have them.
  • Have a list of the medicines you take regularly with you (including blood thinners, diabetes and osteoporosis medication).
  • Tell us about any systemic conditions, allergies and pregnancy before the examination.
  • Arriving a few minutes before your appointment time is enough for registration.

From examination to treatment

  1. 1
    Registration and listening to your complaint

    You are registered at your appointment time; the dentist begins the examination by listening to your complaint, any previous treatment and your general health.

  2. 2
    Intra-oral examination and imaging

    A panoramic X-ray is added to the intra-oral examination when needed. If three-dimensional imaging (CBCT) is required for implant and surgical planning, you are referred for it; the results are assessed at the clinic.

  3. 3
    Sharing the treatment plan

    Based on the examination findings, one or more treatment options are explained, together with the number of sessions required and the approximate duration. You can take time to ask questions and to think before deciding.

  4. 4
    Treatment sessions

    Appointments are made according to the agreed plan. In plans that involve more than one field (for example implant + prosthesis), the surgical and prosthodontic stages are coordinated between the dentists concerned.

  5. 5
    Check-up appointments

    A check-up appointment is planned to monitor healing and the result after treatment; additional check-ups are added if needed.
