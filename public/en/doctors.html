Dt. (DDS) Mehmet Taş

Dentist — implantology and oral surgery

Dentist. Graduated from Istanbul University Faculty of Dentistry in 2009 and completed a master's degree in Oral Surgery and Implantology at UniCamillus University (Rome). At Özel DentTEK he focuses on implant surgery, bone and sinus procedures, implant-supported fixed prostheses for the edentulous jaw (All-on-4 concept) and oral surgery.

He goes through examination and 3D imaging findings together with the patient and explains each option with its advantages and drawbacks. For international patients he carries out the remote preliminary assessment (panoramic X-ray / CT) before arrival.

Areas of practice

  • Dental implant surgery
  • All-on-4 / All-on-6 implant-supported fixed prostheses
  • Bone grafting, sinus lift
  • Impacted and wisdom tooth surgery
  • Zirconia and implant-supported prosthesis planning

Dt. (DDS) Aycan Işık Taş

Dentist — aesthetic and prosthetic dentistry; clinic director

Dentist. Began her studies at Yeditepe University Faculty of Dentistry and graduated from Süleyman Demirel University Faculty of Dentistry in 2014. She has practised at Özel DentTEK since 2014 and serves as the clinic's responsible director. Her work centres on zirconia and all-ceramic restorations, veneers, smile design, implant-supported prostheses and paediatric dentistry.

In aesthetic planning she shows the intended result in advance with photographs and digital design, giving priority to options that preserve tooth structure. She is a member of the Istanbul Dental Chamber (reg. no. 10368) and the Academy of Aesthetic Dentistry (EDAD).

Areas of practice

  • Zirconia and all-ceramic crowns
  • Veneers (porcelain laminates) and smile design
  • Implant-supported prostheses
  • Composite bonding and restorative treatment
  • Paediatric dentistry
