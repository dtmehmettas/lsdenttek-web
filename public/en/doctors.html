Our dentists

Five dentists, five fields of practice: implants and oral surgery, prosthodontic and aesthetic dentistry, endodontics, orthodontics and paediatric dentistry. For treatment plans that involve more than one field, the dentists work together.

MSc, Dt. (DDS) Mehmet Taş

Dentist — implantology and oral surgery

Dentist. Graduated from Istanbul University Faculty of Dentistry in 2009 and completed a master's degree in Oral Surgery and Implantology at UniCamillus University (Rome). At Özel DentTEK he works mainly in implant surgery, bone and sinus procedures, planning of implant-supported fixed prostheses for the edentulous jaw (the All-on-4 concept) and oral surgery.

In planning, he goes through the findings of the examination and three-dimensional imaging together with the patient and explains the options with their advantages and drawbacks. For international patients he carries out the planning before arrival through a remote preliminary assessment (panoramic X-ray / tomography).

Areas of practice

  • Dental implant surgery
  • Implant-supported fixed prostheses with the All-on-4 / All-on-6 concept
  • Bone grafting, sinus lift
  • Impacted tooth and wisdom tooth surgery
  • Planning of zirconia and implant-supported prostheses

Dt. (DDS) Aycan Işık Taş

Dentist — aesthetic and prosthodontic dentistry; responsible director

Dentist. She began her studies at Yeditepe University Faculty of Dentistry and completed them at Süleyman Demirel University Faculty of Dentistry in 2014. She has been seeing patients at Özel DentTEK since 2014 and serves as the clinic's responsible director. Her main areas of work are zirconia and all-ceramic restorations, veneers, smile design, implant-supported prostheses and paediatric dentistry.

In aesthetic planning she shows the intended result in advance with photographs and digital design, and gives priority to options that preserve tooth structure. She is a member of the Istanbul Dental Chamber (reg. no. 10368) and of the Academy of Aesthetic Dentistry Association (EDAD).

Specialist Dt. (DDS) Sebahat Özler Aygörmez

Specialist in endodontics

Specialist in endodontics. She works in root canal treatment, retreatment of teeth that have previously had root canal treatment, and the assessment of pain of dental origin. The aim is to keep in the mouth, as far as possible, a tooth for which extraction has come into question because of advanced tissue loss or infection.

The treatment decision is made on the basis of clinical examination and radiological findings; whether the tooth can be preserved in the long term is assessed together with the restoration to be placed on it.

Areas of practice

  • Root canal treatment (endodontic treatment)
  • Renewal of root canal treatment (retreatment)
  • Follow-up and treatment of traumatised teeth
  • Assessment of pain of dental origin
  • Planning of the restoration after root canal treatment

Education and experience

  • Özel DentTEK Oral and Dental Health Polyclinic, Esenyurt / IstanbulSpecialist in endodontics

Languages: Turkish

Specialist Dt. (DDS) Nida Yaman Zeren

Specialist in orthodontics

Specialist in orthodontics. She graduated from Istanbul University Faculty of Dentistry in 2009 (third in her class) and completed her doctoral training, again at Istanbul University, between 2012 and 2017. Her doctoral thesis was on the retention of lingual brackets (brackets bonded to the back surface of the teeth).

She works on correcting crowding, gaps between teeth and bite problems with fixed braces, a lingual system or clear aligners; monitoring jaw development in children and, when needed, early treatment also fall within her field. The method and duration are determined by examination, models and X-ray assessment, according to the position of the teeth, the jaw relationship and age.

Areas of practice

  • Fixed orthodontic treatment (braces)
  • Orthodontics with clear aligners
  • Lingual (invisible) orthodontics
  • Early (orthopaedic) treatment in children
  • Bringing impacted teeth into the mouth
  • Follow-up of retention after orthodontics

Specialist Dt. (DDS) Ecem Salmaz Utlu

Specialist in paediatric dentistry (paedodontics)

Specialist in paediatric dentistry (paedodontics). She graduated from Istanbul Aydın University Faculty of Dentistry in 2022 and completed her doctoral training in the Department of Paedodontics of the same university. Since 2024 she has been treating child patients at Özel DentTEK.

In the 0–18 age group she carries out the full range of paediatric dental procedures, from preventive procedures (fissure sealants, fluoride) to restorative treatment, from pulp treatments to space maintainers, from management of dental trauma to treatment under sedation and general anaesthesia.

It is as important for a child's first experience of the dentist to be positive as it is to carry out the treatment; procedures are planned according to the child's age and cooperation, and through open communication with families an individual treatment plan is drawn up for each child.

Areas of practice

  • Cavities and restorative treatment in primary and permanent teeth
  • Preventive procedures: fissure sealants, fluoride
  • Pulp treatments in primary teeth (pulpotomy, root canal)
  • Space maintainer planning
  • Management of dental trauma in children
  • Treatment under sedation and general anaesthesia in cases where cooperation cannot be achieved
