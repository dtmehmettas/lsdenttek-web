Frequently asked questions

The most common questions about the team, the process, the transition and enquiries from abroad. Questions specific to a treatment are on the relevant treatment page.

What is LS DentTek?

LS DentTek is the name of the team of MSc, Dt. (DDS) Mehmet Taş and Dt. (DDS) Aycan Işık Taş, who practise within Özel DentTEK Oral and Dental Health Polyclinic. Treatment takes place at the clinic; this site is for information about the team and treatments.

How do I make an appointment?

You can reach us by WhatsApp or telephone, or fill in the contact form; requests are answered during working hours. An examination appointment is arranged at the first contact.

Which dentist carries out which treatment?

Implant surgery, bone and sinus procedures and oral surgery are carried out by MSc, Dt. (DDS) Mehmet Taş; zirconia, veneers, smile design and prosthodontic planning by Dt. (DDS) Aycan Işık Taş; root canal treatment by Specialist Dt. (DDS) Sebahat Özler Aygörmez; braces and clear aligner treatment by Specialist Dt. (DDS) Nida Yaman Zeren; and the treatment of child patients by Specialist Dt. (DDS) Ecem Salmaz Utlu. In treatments with both surgical and prosthodontic stages, such as All-on-4, the dentists work together.

Can I get a plan without travelling from abroad?

A remote preliminary assessment is made from a panoramic X-ray and photographs; the possible options and the time required are shared in writing. The definitive plan is confirmed by the examination at the clinic.

How many visits does treatment require?

Zirconia and smile design can mostly be completed in a single visit of 5–10 days. Implant treatment generally requires two visits (surgery, then the prosthesis 2–4 months later) because of the time the implant needs to fuse with the bone; in suitable cases a temporary fixed tooth can be fitted at the same visit.

What should I bring to the examination?

Your most recent X-rays, if you have them, a list of the medicines you take, and information about your general health (diabetes, blood thinners, osteoporosis medication and so on). These directly affect planning.

Can I come without an appointment?

The clinic works by appointment; if you come without one, you may have to wait depending on how busy the clinic is. In situations such as pain and swelling, letting us know first by telephone or WhatsApp makes it easier to arrange a place for the same day.

I have a toothache, what should I do?

A painkiller is only a temporary measure; the cause of the pain is determined by examination and X-ray. If there is swelling, fever, difficulty swallowing, or a tooth that has shifted after a blow, come in the same day. If a permanent tooth has been knocked out, bring it in milk or saline without touching the root; the first hours are decisive.

Can I bring my child as well?

Yes. Examination and treatment of child patients are carried out by Specialist Dt. (DDS) Ecem Salmaz Utlu, a specialist in paediatric dentistry (paedodontics); the first visit is often just an introduction.

In whose name are the contract and invoice issued?

The treatment contract and invoice are issued in the name of Özel DentTEK Oral and Dental Health Polyclinic, an authorized international health tourism facility. If travel and accommodation are requested, they are provided under a separate contract by the travel partner, Lightstone Group.

Is there parking at the clinic, and how do I get there?

The clinic is at Altıntaş Okutan Park on Nazım Hikmet Boulevard. The clinic has no covered car park of its own; the surrounding streets and open parking areas can be used. For directions you can use the map link on the contact page.

What happened to Lightstone?

Lightstone Group has not closed. Its role has changed from an intermediary organisation to a travel and logistics partner. Treatment services are provided at Özel DentTEK, and travel arrangements by Lightstone Group Travel Agency (TÜRSAB 15898).

Are my earlier appointment / payment still valid?

Your appointment and treatment records remain valid. The treatment contract and invoice are issued in the name of Özel DentTEK, an authorized international health tourism facility. You can get details from our contact line.

Who will I be dealing with?

For treatment and information from the dentists you will deal directly with the team of Dt. (DDS) Mehmet Taş and Dt. (DDS) Aycan Işık Taş at Özel DentTEK; for travel and logistics, with Lightstone Group Travel Agency.

Dental Implant Treatment

A titanium root placed in the jawbone in place of a missing tooth, carrying a fixed crown, bridge or denture; neighbouring teeth are left untouched.

Performed by: MSc, Dt. (DDS) Mehmet TaşDetails →

The All-on-4 Concept

A fixed prosthesis on four (if needed six) implants in a completely edentulous jaw; when bone is insufficient, the posterior implants are placed at an angle.

Performed by: MSc, Dt. (DDS) Mehmet Taş & Dt. (DDS) Aycan Işık TaşDetails →

Zirconia Crowns

A ceramic restoration that contains no metal and whose translucency is close to that of a natural tooth; for teeth with large fillings, root canal treatment or a colour problem.

Performed by: Dt. (DDS) Aycan Işık TaşDetails →

Smile Design

Aesthetic planning in which tooth and facial proportions are assessed together, with a digital preview; veneers, zirconia, bonding and gum contouring.

Performed by: Dt. (DDS) Aycan Işık Taş & MSc, Dt. (DDS) Mehmet TaşDetails →

Root Canal Treatment

Saving a tooth from extraction by cleaning out an inflamed or non-vital dental nerve and filling the canals.

Performed by: Specialist Dt. (DDS) Sebahat Özler AygörmezDetails →

Orthodontics

Aligning crowded, spaced or poorly occluding teeth with fixed braces or clear aligner treatment; also carried out in adults.

Performed by: Specialist Dt. (DDS) Nida Yaman ZerenDetails →

Paediatric Dentistry

Preserving primary teeth, fissure sealants and fluoride applications, treatment of cavities and a first visit that passes without fear.

Performed by: Specialist Dt. (DDS) Ecem Salmaz UtluDetails →

Teeth Whitening

An aesthetic procedure that lightens discolouration in the enamel and dentin with a peroxide-based gel, without removing any tooth structure.
