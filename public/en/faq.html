Frequently asked questions

The most common questions about the team, the process, the transition and enquiries from abroad. Treatment-specific questions are on each treatment page.

What is LS DentTek?

LS DentTek is the name of the team of Dt. Mehmet Taş and Dt. Aycan Işık Taş practising within Özel DentTEK Oral and Dental Health Polyclinic, a facility licensed for international health tourism. Treatment takes place at the clinic; this site provides information about the team and treatments.

How do I make an appointment?

Write on WhatsApp, call, or fill in the contact form; enquiries are answered during working hours. An examination appointment is arranged at the first contact.

Which dentist does which treatment?

Implant surgery, bone and sinus procedures and oral surgery are carried out by Dt. Mehmet Taş; zirconia, veneers, smile design and prosthetic planning by Dt. Aycan Işık Taş. For treatments with surgical and prosthetic stages, such as All-on-4, the two dentists work together.

Can I get a plan before travelling?

A preliminary assessment is made remotely from a panoramic X-ray and photographs; possible options and the time required are shared in writing. The final plan is confirmed at the examination in the clinic.

How many visits does treatment take?

Zirconia crowns and smile design can usually be completed in a single visit of 5–10 days. Implant treatment generally needs two visits (surgery, and the prosthesis 2–4 months later) because of the fusion period; in suitable cases a temporary fixed tooth is fitted at the first visit.

What should I bring to the examination?

Any recent X-rays, a list of the medicines you take and information about your general health (diabetes, blood thinners, osteoporosis medicines, etc.). These directly affect planning.

What happened to Lightstone?

Lightstone Group is not closed. Its role changed from intermediary institution to travel and logistics partner. Treatment services are provided at Özel DentTEK, travel arrangements by Lightstone Group Travel Agency (TÜRSAB 15898).

Is my old appointment / payment still valid?

Your appointment and treatment records remain valid. Treatment contract and invoice are issued in the name of Özel DentTEK Oral and Dental Health Polyclinic, an authorized international health tourism facility. Please contact us for details.

Who will I be in contact with?

For treatment and dentist information, you will be in contact directly with the Dt. (DDS) Mehmet Taş and Dt. (DDS) Aycan Işık Taş team at Özel DentTEK, and for travel and logistics matters with Lightstone Group Travel Agency.

Dental Implants

A titanium root placed in the jawbone to replace a missing tooth, carrying a fixed crown, bridge or denture; neighbouring teeth are left untouched.

All-on-4 Concept

A fixed prosthesis on four (or six) implants in a fully edentulous jaw; the back implants are angled to make use of available bone.

Zirconia Crowns
