Information pages

The information below is for general guidance. Which treatment is right for you can only be decided after an examination.

Dental Implants

A titanium root placed in the jawbone to replace a missing tooth, carrying a fixed crown, bridge or denture; neighbouring teeth are left untouched.

Performed by: Dt. Mehmet TaşRead more →

All-on-4 Concept

A fixed prosthesis on four (or six) implants in a fully edentulous jaw; the back implants are angled to make use of available bone.

Performed by: Dt. Mehmet Taş & Dt. Aycan Işık TaşRead more →

Zirconia Crowns

Metal-free ceramic crowns with light transmission close to natural teeth; for heavily filled, root-treated or discoloured teeth.

Performed by: Dt. Aycan Işık TaşRead more →

Smile Design

Aesthetic planning that considers teeth and facial proportions together, with a digital preview; veneers, zirconia, bonding and gum contouring.

Performed by: Dt. Aycan Işık Taş & Dt. Mehmet TaşRead more →

Approach

How we work

  1. 1
    Examination and imaging

    Panoramic X-ray, 3D tomography where needed and intra-oral photos are recorded. For enquiries from abroad this stage begins with a remote preliminary assessment.

  2. 2
    Planning together

    Options are explained with their advantages and drawbacks; duration, number of visits and maintenance are discussed from the start. Decisions are made together with the patient.

  3. 3
    Treatment and follow-up

    Surgical and prosthetic stages are coordinated by the two dentists. Check-ups and home care are part of keeping the result.
