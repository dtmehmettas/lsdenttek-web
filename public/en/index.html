Treatments

Information pages

The information below is for general guidance only; the treatment that suits you can only be decided after an examination.

Dental Implant Treatment

A titanium root placed in the jawbone in place of a missing tooth, carrying a fixed crown, bridge or denture; neighbouring teeth are left untouched.

Performed by: Mehmet Taş, DDS, MScDetails →

The All-on-4 Concept

A fixed prosthesis on four (if needed six) implants in a completely edentulous jaw; when bone is insufficient, the posterior implants are placed at an angle.

Performed by: Mehmet Taş, DDS, MSc & Aycan Işık Taş, DDSDetails →

Zirconia Crowns

A ceramic restoration that contains no metal and whose translucency is close to that of a natural tooth; for teeth with large fillings, root canal treatment or a colour problem.

Performed by: Aycan Işık Taş, DDSDetails →

Smile Design

Aesthetic planning in which tooth and facial proportions are assessed together, with a digital preview; veneers, zirconia, bonding and gum contouring.

Performed by: Aycan Işık Taş, DDS & Mehmet Taş, DDS, MScDetails →

Root Canal Treatment

Saving a tooth from extraction by cleaning out an inflamed or non-vital dental nerve and filling the canals.

Performed by: Sebahat Özler Aygörmez, DDS (Endodontist)Details →

Orthodontics

Aligning crowded, spaced or poorly occluding teeth with fixed braces or clear aligner treatment; also carried out in adults.

Performed by: Nida Yaman Zeren, DDS (Orthodontist)Details →

Paediatric Dentistry

Preserving primary teeth, fissure sealants and fluoride applications, treatment of cavities and a calm, reassuring first visit.

Performed by: Ecem Salmaz Utlu, DDS (Paediatric Dentist)Details →

Teeth Whitening

An aesthetic procedure that lightens discolouration in the enamel and dentin with a peroxide-based gel, without removing any tooth structure.

Performed by: Aycan Işık Taş, DDSDetails →

Veneers (Porcelain Laminates)

A very thin porcelain shell bonded only to the front surface of the tooth; an aesthetic option that preserves as much tooth structure as possible for colour, shape and spacing concerns.

Performed by: Aycan Işık Taş, DDSDetails →

Bone Grafting and Sinus Lift

Preparatory surgery that supports jawbone lost after tooth loss with a graft and, in the upper back jaw, raises the sinus floor; it creates the foundation on which the implant will sit.

Performed by: Mehmet Taş, DDS, MScDetails →

Impacted Teeth and Wisdom Tooth Surgery

Examination, imaging and, if needed, surgical removal under local anaesthesia of teeth that stay within the jawbone or gum when they should have erupted; not every impacted tooth is removed, and some are monitored.

Performed by: Mehmet Taş, DDS, MScDetails →

Gum Disease Treatment

Treatment aimed at stopping gum inflammation and the loss of the bone and fibres that hold the tooth, made up of professional cleaning, scaling and root planing, surgery if needed, and regular maintenance appointments.

Performed by: Mehmet Taş, DDS, MSc & Aycan Işık Taş, DDSDetails →

Emergency Toothache

First response to sudden toothache, swelling, or a broken or knocked-out tooth; finding the cause and treating the source.

Performed by: Mehmet Taş, DDS, MScDetails →

Approach

How we work

  1. 1
    Examination and imaging

    Findings are recorded with a panoramic X-ray, three-dimensional tomography (CBCT) where needed, and intra-oral photographs. For enquiries from abroad, this stage begins with a remote preliminary assessment.

  2. 2
    Planning together

    Options are explained with their advantages and drawbacks; duration, number of visits and maintenance requirements are discussed from the start. The decision is made together with the patient.

  3. 3
    Treatment and follow-up

    In plans that involve more than one field, the surgical, prosthodontic, endodontic and orthodontic stages are coordinated between the dentists. Check-up appointments and home care are part of keeping the result.
