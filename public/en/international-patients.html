Özel DentTEK — authorized international health tourism facility (Turkish Ministry of Health)

How it works for patients from abroad

Özel DentTEK Oral and Dental Health Polyclinic is an authorized international health tourism facility (Turkish Ministry of Health). Treatment and travel are handled separately; for treatment, the patient deals directly with the health facility.

Treatment — Özel DentTEK

Examination, planning and treatment services are carried out at Özel DentTEK Oral and Dental Health Polyclinic, an authorized international health tourism facility, by the Dt. (DDS) Mehmet Taş and Dt. (DDS) Aycan Işık Taş team after examination. Contracts and invoices are issued in the clinic's name; the patient deals directly with the health facility.

Travel — Lightstone Group Travel Agency

Travel and logistics services such as transfer, accommodation, interpreter and flight arrangements are provided as travel partner by Lightstone Group Travel Agency (TÜRSAB 15898). These services are not provided by the clinic. Travel arrangements are discussed directly with Lightstone.

Step by step

  1. 1
    Get in touch

    Write on WhatsApp, by e-mail or through the form. Describe your complaint and send any existing X-rays and photos. We reply in Turkish and English.

  2. 2
    Remote preliminary assessment

    The dentists make a preliminary assessment from a panoramic X-ray (CT if available) and intra-oral photos. This does not replace an examination; the final plan is confirmed at the examination in the clinic.

  3. 3
    Plan and schedule

    Possible treatment options, the number of visits and the time needed in Istanbul are shared in writing. Implant treatment usually requires two separate visits (surgery and prosthesis).

  4. 4
    Travel arrangements

    If you wish, flights, accommodation and transfers are arranged by Lightstone Group Travel Agency. The treatment schedule and the travel plan are set up together.

  5. 5
    Examination and treatment

    Examination in the clinic, CT if needed and the final plan; then the treatment sessions. Surgical and prosthetic stages are coordinated by the two dentists.

  6. 6
    Follow-up

    After you return home, contact continues for check-ups and questions; you receive a treatment summary to share with your local dentist.

Good to know

  • A personal plan is only made after examination and imaging; the remote assessment is preliminary information only.
  • Some treatments (for example the prosthesis after implants) need healing time and may not be completed in a single visit.
  • Contracts, invoices and medical records are issued in the name of Özel DentTEK. Travel services are provided by Lightstone under a separate agreement.
  • Your personal and health data are processed under Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and health legislation.
