Travel partner

Lightstone Group

Lightstone Group Travel Agency (TÜRSAB licence no. 15898) is the travel and logistics partner of the LS DentTek team. Transfers, accommodation, interpreters and flights for patients from abroad are arranged by Lightstone.

Who does what

Treatment: provided within Özel DentTEK Oral and Dental Health Polyclinic, a facility licensed for international health tourism, by the team of Dt. Mehmet Taş and Dt. Aycan Işık Taş. Contracts and invoices are issued in the clinic's name.

Travel and logistics: Lightstone Group Travel Agency (TÜRSAB 15898) — transfers, accommodation, interpreters and flights. These services are not provided by the clinic.

From Lightstone Group to LS DentTek

Lightstone Group began as an intermediary organisation; when Özel DentTEK obtained its own international health tourism licence, Lightstone's role became that of travel and logistics partner. The team and the dentists are the same; treatment now takes place directly at the licensed facility. The "LS" in the name comes from Lightstone, and the brand keeps that link.

The old pages at lightstonegroup.com.tr redirect to this site. Appointments and treatment records of former patients remain valid; please use our contact line for any questions.

Frequently asked questions

What happened to Lightstone?

Lightstone Group is not closed. Its role changed from intermediary institution to travel and logistics partner. Treatment services are provided at Özel DentTEK, travel arrangements by Lightstone Group Travel Agency (TÜRSAB 15898).

Is my old appointment / payment still valid?

Your appointment and treatment records remain valid. Treatment contract and invoice are issued in the name of Özel DentTEK Oral and Dental Health Polyclinic, an authorized international health tourism facility. Please contact us for details.

Who will I be in contact with?

For treatment and dentist information, you will be in contact directly with the Dt. (DDS) Mehmet Taş and Dt. (DDS) Aycan Işık Taş team at Özel DentTEK, and for travel and logistics matters with Lightstone Group Travel Agency.
