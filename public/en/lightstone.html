Travel partner

Lightstone Group

Lightstone Group Travel Agency (TÜRSAB Licence No: 15898) is the travel and logistics partner of the LS DentTek team. Transfer, accommodation, interpreter and flight arrangements for patients coming from abroad are made by Lightstone.

Division of roles

Treatment: Provided at Özel Denttek Ağız ve Diş Sağlığı Polikliniği (Polyclinic for Oral and Dental Health) by the team of Mehmet Taş, DDS, MSc, and Aycan Işık Taş, DDS. The contract and invoice are issued in the name of the clinic.

Travel and logistics: Lightstone Group Travel Agency (TÜRSAB 15898) — transfer, accommodation, interpreter and flight arrangements. These services are not provided by the clinic.

From Lightstone Group to LS DentTek

Lightstone Group began as an intermediary organisation; when Özel Denttek obtained its own International Health Tourism Authorisation Certificate, Lightstone's role became that of travel and logistics partner. The team and the dentists are the same; treatment is now provided directly at the authorised facility. The “LS” in the name comes from Lightstone, and the brand keeps that link.

The old pages at lightstonegroup.com.tr redirect to this site. The appointment and treatment records of our former patients remain valid; you can use our contact line for any questions.

Frequently asked questions

What happened to Lightstone?

Lightstone Group has not closed. Its role has changed from an intermediary organisation to a travel and logistics partner. Treatment services are provided at Özel Denttek, and travel arrangements by Lightstone Group Travel Agency (TÜRSAB 15898).

Are my earlier appointment / payment still valid?

Your appointment and treatment records remain valid. The treatment contract and invoice are issued in the name of Özel Denttek, an authorised international health tourism facility. You can get details from our contact line.

Who will I be dealing with?

For treatment and information from the dentists you will deal directly with the team of Mehmet Taş, DDS, and Aycan Işık Taş, DDS, at Özel Denttek; for travel and logistics, with Lightstone Group Travel Agency.
