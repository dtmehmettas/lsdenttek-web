Privacy notice

This notice is issued under Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK) by Özel DentTEK Ağız ve Diş Sağlığı Polikliniği (Cumhuriyet Mah. Nazım Hikmet Blv. No:89/2, Altıntaş Okutan İş Merkezi giriş katı, 34515 Esenyurt / İstanbul) as data controller. This website is the information site of the team of Dt. Mehmet Taş and Dt. Aycan Işık Taş practising within the polyclinic.

1. Data processed

The name, phone number, e-mail address, country, subject and optional note you send through the contact form, e-mail or WhatsApp, together with the date of submission and IP address, are processed. X-rays and intra-oral photographs sent for a remote preliminary assessment are health data and are examined only by the dentists, solely for that assessment.

2. Purpose and legal basis

Data are processed to evaluate your enquiry or appointment request and to contact you, under KVKK Art. 5/2-c (necessary for the conclusion or performance of a contract) and Art. 5/2-f (legitimate interest of the data controller). Health data are processed under KVKK Art. 6 and health legislation, with your explicit consent or in the cases provided by law.

3. Transfers

Form data are stored on the server hosting the site (Natro, Türkiye) and forwarded to the clinic's e-mail. If you request travel arrangements, only your name, contact details and travel dates are shared, with your explicit consent, with our travel partner Lightstone Group Travel Agency; health data are not shared. Data are not shared with third parties except where required by law and are not used for advertising or marketing.

4. Retention

Enquiry records are deleted no later than one year after the enquiry is closed. Patient file records are subject to the retention periods in health legislation.

5. Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to learn whether your personal data are processed, to request information, to learn the purpose and whether data are used accordingly, to request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, to request deletion or destruction, to learn the third parties to whom data are transferred and to claim compensation for damage. Requests may be sent to info@lsdenttek.com.tr or in writing to the clinic address; they are answered within 30 days at the latest.

6. Cookies

This site keeps only a local record in your browser to remember that you closed the top information bar; it uses no tracking cookies. The embedded Google Maps component is subject to Google's own cookie and privacy policy; the address is available on the page without viewing the map.
